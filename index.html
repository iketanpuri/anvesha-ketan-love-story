04 · Words I mean with all my heart

For my Guggu.

Kuch baatein jo dil mein hain...

Meri pyaari Anvesha,

Yaar Guggu, pata hai? Kabhi kabhi main bas yun hi tumhare baare mein sochne lagta hoon aur apne aap smile aa jaati hai. Life mein kitne saare log milte hain, lekin tumhare saath jo apnapan feel hota hai, woh mere liye bahut special hai. Tumhare saath ek normal sa din bhi aisi memory ban jaata hai jise main baar baar yaad karna chahta hoon.

Mujhe sirf humare perfect photos ya special moments se pyaar nahi hai. Mujhe humari silly selfies, bina kisi baat ke hasna, ek dusre ko dekh kar smile karna aur saath chalte hue chhoti chhoti baatein karna bhi utna hi pasand hai. Tumhare saath kuch extraordinary karna zaroori nahi lagta — tum saath ho, toh simple moments bhi kaafi hote hain.

Tumhari smile, tumhari hasi aur tumhare woh cute se expressions… sab mere dil ke bahut kareeb hain. Kabhi tumhari koi baat yaad aa jaati hai, kabhi humari koi photo dekh leta hoon, aur din thoda aur achha lagne lagta hai. I love how just the thought of you can make an ordinary moment feel warmer.

Main shayad har baar apni feelings sahi words mein express nahi kar pata. Kabhi mazaak mein baat nikal jaati hai, kabhi jo bolna hota hai woh dil mein hi reh jaata hai. Isliye aaj clearly kehna chahta hoon: tum mere liye bahut matter karti ho, Anvesha. Tumhare saath bitaya hua time mere liye precious hai, aur main tumhe apni life mein paakar bahut khush hoon.

Aur Guggu, tumhe mere saamne hamesha happy ya perfectly sorted rehne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Kabhi din achha na ho, mood off ho, ya bas kisi se baat karne ka mann ho, toh main chahta hoon ki tum mujhse apne dil ki baat keh sako. Har problem ka answer shayad mere paas na ho, lekin tumhari baat dhyaan se sunna aur tumhe samajhne ki koshish karna mere liye hamesha important rahega.

Main chahta hoon ki hum ek dusre ko aur achhe se samjhein, chhoti chhoti khushiyan celebrate karein, aur jab kabhi koi baat uljhe toh pyaar se usse suljha sakein. Hum dono ki apni feelings, apne dreams aur apne thode silly sides hain — aur mujhe humara saath aise hi, dheere dheere aur khoobsurat banate rehna hai.

Abhi toh tumhare saath aur bahut saari memories banani hain. Aur photos click karni hain, random plans banane hain, bina wajah hasna hai, aur aise moments jeene hain jinhe baad mein dekh kar hum bolein, “Yaar, kitna pyaara din tha!” Koi grand plan ho ya bas saath baith kar baatein karna, meri favourite part tumhara saath hi rahega.

Yeh chhota sa surprise bhi bas meri taraf se tumhe smile karane ki ek koshish hai. Jab bhi ise kholo, toh yaad rakhna ki kisi ke liye tumhari hasi, tumhari baatein aur tumhara hona bahut special hai. Thank you, meri life mein itni pyaari memories aur itni saari smiles laane ke liye.

Love you, meri Guggu. Tumhare liye ek extra tight hug, aur humari aane wali saari pyaari memories ke naam ek badi si smile. ❤️

Hamesha tumhara,
Ketan ♡
